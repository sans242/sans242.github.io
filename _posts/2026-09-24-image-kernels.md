Here is an awesome interactive site to explore and visualize how image kernels work:

👉 **[Image Kernels Explained Visually](https://setosa.io/ev/image-kernels/)**

Image kernels (or convolution matrices) are small matrices used to apply visual effects like blurring, sharpening, embossing, edge detection, and more to images. This interactive tool provides an intuitive, hands-on breakdown of how pixel transformations happen in real-time.
